What Updock does

Updock keeps your Docker containers on their newest images, like Watchtower, with a web UI: it checks registries on a schedule, updates containers in place (recreating them with the same settings), verifies they come back healthy and rolls back if they don't. You can check, update, pin, roll back or deploy another version of any container right away from the Containers tab.
